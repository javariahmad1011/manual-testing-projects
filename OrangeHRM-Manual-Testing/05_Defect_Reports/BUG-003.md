## Summary

The Recruitment candidate form did not satisfy the expected invalid-email validation behavior during execution of `TC-REC-005`.

## Preconditions

- Authorized user is authenticated.
- Recruitment module is accessible.
- **Candidates → Add Candidate** form is open.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. Open **Recruitment → Candidates**.
2. Select **Add**.
3. Enter all required candidate details.
4. Enter `john.smith` in the email field.
5. Click **Save**.
6. Observe whether the form blocks submission and displays format validation.

## Actual Result

The historical execution was recorded as **Fail** because the malformed email scenario did not satisfy the expected validation rule.

The original execution record did not retain whether the record was saved, whether Save remained enabled, or the exact validation text displayed.

## Expected Result

The form should reject `john.smith` as an invalid email address and display a clear format-validation message without saving the candidate record.

## Business / User Impact

Weak email validation can introduce unusable or inaccurate candidate contact data into the recruitment process and may cause downstream communication failures.
